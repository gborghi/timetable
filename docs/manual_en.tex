\noindent\small\rmfamily
\textsc{This English edition} is set in EB Garamond for the body
text --- with old-style figures and authentic small caps ---,
in Lato for the few labels of schematic figures, and in
Inconsolata for code blocks. The page proportions follow the
classical canon of Bringhurst and Tschichold: ample outer margin
for marginalia, narrower inner margin, airy head, ornamented foot.
Each chapter opens with a three-line drop cap; the chapter number
is set in Roman numerals, the title in small caps framed by
horizontal rules and by a four-leaf rosette
(\textsc{decofour}, from the \texttt{fourier-orns} package).

\bigskip
\noindent\textsc{Compilation}: \texttt{lualatex} (\TeX{} Live 2024),
\texttt{biber} for the bibliography, \texttt{makeindex} for the
analytical index. The build pipeline lives in
\texttt{docs/build\_manual.sh} which emits \textbf{both}
\texttt{manual.pdf} (Italian) and \texttt{manual\_en.pdf} (English)
in a single invocation.

\smallskip
\noindent\textsc{Note on translation}: the Italian edition remains
the primary reference. This English edition fully translates the
introduction, the BP-related chapters and the benchmark chapter;
the other chapters carry an English summary that points to the
corresponding Italian chapter for the full narrative. Where
Italian-school context is necessary (for instance the legal frame
of the \emph{contratto collettivo nazionale}, the role of the
\emph{insegnante di sostegno}, or the meaning of \emph{indirizzo
di studio}), explanatory notes are added in the English text.
